I am a fourth year PhD student in Computer Science, working with [Ethan
Cecchetti](https://cecchetti.sites.cs.wisc.edu/) on Choreographic
Programming. My work seeks to enable the construction of real choreographic
systems while maintaining the strong theoretical properties of choreographic
languages. Before that I was advised by [Tej Chajed](https://chajed.io) and
worked on software verification for distributed systems. I am also passionate
about teaching and computer science education, and I taught CS 400 during the
summer.

More detailed information can be found on the [About](about) page.
